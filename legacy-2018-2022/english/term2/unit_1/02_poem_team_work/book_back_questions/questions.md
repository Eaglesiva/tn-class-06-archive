### Poem - D. Read the lines and answer

*Type: Poem (Line-based questions) | Source: page_27*

D. Read the lines and answer the questions given below.

1. It's helping your fellowman to score When his chances hopeless seem; Its forgetting self till the game is o're And fighting for the team.
   a) What does 'it' stand for here?
   b) Write the rhyme scheme for the above lines.

2. They may sound your praise and call you great, They may single you out for fame, But you must work with your running mate Or you'll never win the game;
   a) Whom does 'they' refer to?
   b) Which line talks about team spirit?
   c) Pick out the rhyming words from the given lines.
